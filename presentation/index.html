04The demo · what runs, and how fast
Every scored number, in four steps
1 BLEND inverse-variance, log space log m = Σwᵢ·log mᵢ / Σwᵢ , w=1/σᵢ² σ_mem 0.43 σ_model 0.60 2 POSTERIOR coverage = mass at zero P(t) = (1−p)·δ(0) + p·LogN(log m, σ) 3 CHARGE maximise k·P(t ≥ k·m) a = k(σ)·m, k = 0.85 − 0.45σ 4 LIMIT accept iff P(fair) > ⅔ b = Q⅓(P) → p ≤ ⅔ ⇒ b = 0 by itself

The ⅓ is derived, not tuned: rejecting a fair claim costs 1.5a, a fraudulent one costs nothing.

Unattended · 100 Games · one every 12.6 min
T+0s blind floor — posted before the key T+3s key · decrypt · parse the invoice T+50s two model draws → engine → merged per Line Item T+60s window closes · decision log written

Half the tournament ran while everyone was asleep. Break-even uptime is 71 % — showing up is 2.5× being right.

a = 0, b = 0 does not score zero. b = 0 wrongfully rejects every fair claim, so a dark team pays 1.5a to every awake opponent. We watched it happen: makalu paid us 179,993 and collected 0.00 from anyone.
