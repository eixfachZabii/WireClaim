===============================================================================
BICYCLE THEFT INSURANCE - POLICY CONDITIONS
===============================================================================

1. PURPOSE

This policy insures the bicycle described in the schedule against theft. The
insurer indemnifies the policyholder for the financial loss suffered when the
insured bicycle is stolen.

2. INSURED EVENT

An insured event is the theft of the insured bicycle, that is, its removal by a
third party with the intent of unlawfully keeping it.

3. CONDITIONS OF COVER

Cover applies only where, at the time of the theft, the bicycle was secured with
a lock to a fixed object (a bike stand, railing, lamp post or comparable fixed
structure), or was kept in a locked room.

4. AMOUNT OF INDEMNITY

The insurer reimburses the market value of the bicycle at the time of the theft,
up to the sum insured stated in the schedule.

5. EXCLUSIONS

Not covered:
  (a) theft of a bicycle that was left unlocked;
  (b) loss of, or damage to, accessories carried loosely (lights, bags, pumps);
  (c) theft caused intentionally by the policyholder.

6. POLICYHOLDER DUTIES

The policyholder must report the theft to the police without delay and provide
the insurer with the crime reference and proof of the bicycle's value.
